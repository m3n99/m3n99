## About me

I build mobile products that thousands of people use every day, and I lead the team that ships them. Seven years deep in **Flutter/Dart** — BLoC architecture, CI/CD, App Store and Play releases, secure storage — and now pushing the same production discipline into **AI systems**: retrieval pipelines, agents, and evaluation.

```yaml
role:      Senior Software Engineer & Team Lead
core:      Flutter · Dart · BLoC · Clean Architecture
expanding: NestJS · TypeScript · LLM applications
building:  Arabic-first AI tools for the MENA region
languages: العربية (native) · English (fluent)
```

- Shipping enterprise mobile apps and mentoring the engineers who build them
- Studying applied AI engineering — agents, RAG, fine-tuning, evaluation, production inference
- Most interested in the gap between a demo that works and a system that stays up
- Open to collaborations, freelance work, or a conversation about Arabic-language AI
